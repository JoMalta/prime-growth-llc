Privacy Policy

How Prime Growth LLC handles information collected through this website.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Prime Growth LLC (“the Company”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses and protects information when you visit this website or contact us. By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you provide, such as your name, company, e-mail address, phone number and the content of messages you send us by e-mail.
  • Technical information processed automatically when you visit, such as browser type, device information, IP address, pages visited and time of access. Our hosting provider may process this data for security and performance.

This website has no user accounts, online payments or checkout.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and other communications;
  • To provide information about our services;
  • To maintain, secure and improve the website;
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

3. Cookies and local storage

This website uses essential browser storage (localStorage) to remember your cookie preference. We do not use advertising cookies. Fonts are served from this website, so no third-party font service receives your visit. If analytics tools are added in the future, this policy will be updated.

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies in the cookie banner, and clear or block storage in your browser settings.

4. Sharing of information

  • With service providers that support our operations, such as hosting and e-mail providers;
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process or governmental request;
  • To protect the rights, property or safety of the Company, our clients or others;
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. Data security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

6. Data retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

7. Your choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided, subject to applicable law.

8. Third-party links

This website may link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

9. Children’s privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new date.
